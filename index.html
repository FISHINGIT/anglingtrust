The five dashboards

Each one follows the same pattern: an Overview for the headline, a Detail register you can filter and search, and a third view that answers the question colleagues most often ask. Filters recalculate every figure live, and every dashboard can be saved as a PDF from the top bar.

How they fit together

Read left to right and the five dashboards trace the route a newcomer takes into angling, the support the team puts in place at each step, and the work that keeps the waters worth fishing.

Coach Bursaries

Train the people

Environment Agency Coach Bursaries help more people start fishing, develop angling skills and run safe, enjoyable and well-structured coaching sessions.

Get Fishing Fund

Fund the sessions

Grants to clubs, fisheries and community groups pay for the taster events, kit and bait that bring first-timers to the water.

Rod Licence Waivers

Remove the barrier

Waivers issued by Regional Officers let newcomers fish legally at an organised event without buying a licence first.

Reel Education

Reach the schools

Delivery partners take angling into primary and secondary schools, and Access to Angling grants help schools turn a taster into a club.

Fishery Management Advisors

Look after the waters

Advisors help clubs and fisheries with predation, fencing, licences and improvement funding, so the waters stay worth fishing.

How to use dashboards

Tips to find your way around...

Regions and EA Areas

Every dashboard maps to the eight Angling Trust Regions (London sits within South East) and the fourteen Environment Agency Areas. Click a Region on the map to filter; use Reset to clear.

Figures update on-screen

Each figure updates from the register rows in your browser, so a filtered KPI, chart and table always agree with each other.

People shown appropriately

Organisers, clubs, schools and delivery partners appear in full as professional roles. Officers appear as initials. Participants, applicants and school contacts are never shown.

Data source

Each dashboard reads its own data source, usually an Excel workbook kept by each Department. When the workbook is updated to GitHub, the next refresh rewrites the dashboard.

Dates and periods

Financial quarters are April to March. Period labels are in the column headers.

Save as a PDF

Use the Download PDF button in a dashboard's top bar to capture the current filtered view for a report, board paper or email.
